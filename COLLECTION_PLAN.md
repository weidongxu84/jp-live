# Collection Plan — Japanese Artist Lives in Hong Kong (Aug-Nov 2026)

How we discover, verify, and record live event information from **public sources**.
Target: Japanese singers, voice actors/actresses (声優), and bands performing in
**Hong Kong** during **August through November 2026**.

---

## 1. Source list (where to look)

### A. Official / primary sources (highest trust)
- **Artist official sites** — "LIVE" / "SCHEDULE" / "TOUR" / "NEWS" pages.
- **Artist official socials** — X/Twitter, Instagram, YouTube (community posts),
  official fan clubs, LINE / Weibo (for overseas shows).
- **Record label / agency sites** — tour announcements often land here first.
- **Overseas-show promoter announcements** (see below).

### B. Ticketing platforms serving Hong Kong (authoritative for on-sale/venue)
- **Cityline** (cityline.com) — major HK ticketing.
- **URBTIX** (urbtix.hk) — government/public venues.
- **KKTIX** (kktix.cc) — widely used for anisong / niche Japanese lives (app-only
  dynamic-QR tickets); search organizer subdomains, e.g. `edproduction.kktix.cc`.
- **Klook / Ticketflap / Bookyay / Galaxy Macau & regional platforms** — event pages.
- Artist-specific / promoter-specific ticketing microsites.

### C. Local HK promoters & venues (concert organizers)
- **Anisong / Japanese-live promoters** — these announce smaller livehouse shows the
  big aggregators miss. Known examples: **ED Production** (ed-production.com.hk),
  **Sunny Side Up (光尚娛樂)**, Sony Music HK. Search "主辦" / "presents" / "香港站".
- Promoters who regularly bring Japanese acts to HK.
- **Venues (arena → livehouse):** AsiaWorld-Expo, Hong Kong Coliseum (紅館),
  KITEC / Star Hall, Queen Elizabeth Stadium, MacPherson Stadium, TIDES (Hung Hom),
  and smaller livehouses (e.g. **PORTAL / The Burrow**, This Town Needs / TTN,
  Rotunda 3) — check each venue's event calendar.

### D. Aggregators & community (leads only — must be verified)
- **Japan-focused HK concert trackers** (e.g. thehkhub, jpop-tour.live, Trip.com
  concert collections) — good coverage of arena acts, weak on livehouse anisong.
- **Japanese live databases** — e.g. **eventernote.com** (per-artist tour listings),
  artist official "LIVE/SCHEDULE" pages (e.g. true-singer.com) for overseas dates.
- **Anime/live-event aggregators**, J-pop event trackers.
- **Reddit** (r/jpop, r/anime, city subreddits), **Discord** fan servers.
- Fan-run calendars / spreadsheets, HK anime & J-culture Facebook groups.
- Convention lineups (anime/game conventions in HK announcing guest 声優).

### E. HK event-listing platforms & fan aggregators (PRIMARY for anisong/seiyuu)
> **These are the highest-yield discovery channel for seiyuu/anisong/livehouse
> shows** — the smaller events that arena aggregators and general web search miss.
> Poll them first. Each listing links to an official/ticketing page to verify against.
- **art-mate.net** — HK arts/ACG event listings; carries most JP anisong/seiyuu lives
  (browse the 音樂 / ACG categories). Often the organizer's own listing page.
- **timable.com** — HK event calendar (EN/繁/简); reliable for JP fan-meets & lives.
- **ACGEvent.com** (acgevent.com) — HK ACG music/live-event news; strong seiyuu coverage.
  **Often breaks date/venue *before* tickets go on sale** — poll its news feed, not just
  ticketing sites.
- **shadowzo.com** — HK ACG news site; announces JP artist HK shows.
- **eventernote.com** — search each act's page **and** its HK/overseas events (e.g. an
  "Live in HK" event page appears here early, before local ticketing is set up).
- **巴哈姆特 (forum.gamer.com.tw) per-anime boards** (哈啦板) — for currently-airing/hot
  anime IP acts, the fan board is often the *first* place a HK date/venue is confirmed.
- **HK ticketing carrying these shows:** **UUTIX** (uutix.com), **KKTIX** (organizer
  subdomains), **art-mate**, **Cityline**, **Ticketflap**, **Neon Lit**,
  **bilibili 会员购 / show.bilibili.com** (for mainland-facing sales).
- **TicketHK** (tickethk.com), **hk01 娛樂** — media guides that corroborate dates/prices.

### F. General fan-made aggregators & databases (leads only — must be verified)
Broadly used community trackers. Treat all as **unverified leads**: every hit must be
confirmed against an official artist/promoter/ticketing source before it is recorded.
- **LiveFans** (livefans.jp) — large JP live/festival database with per-artist alerts;
  sometimes lists overseas dates.
- **eventernote.com** — per-artist live listings (good for anisong).
- **AniSong Headline** (anisong.org) — fan-run anisong/seiyuu news incl. HK/Asia stops.
- **Japanese Music Tour Calendar** (electricbloomwebzine.com, ex-JROCK News) —
  filterable global JP tour calendar (J-rock/visual-kei skew, filter for pop/anisong).
- **CDJapan "J-POP Goes Global"** yearly feature — world-tour roundup incl. Asia dates.
- **HMV&BOOKS / Neowing** overseas-performance roundup articles (Japanese).
- **Trip.com HK concerts collection** (jp.trip.com) — JP-language UI, arena skew.
- **discuss.com.hk** (動漫板), **Reddit** r/HongKong event threads.
- Fan-maintained **Notion / Google Calendar** ("J-Pop World Tour 2026", "Asia Tour Calendar").

### G. Social media (fast, early — must be verified)
Often the earliest signal; always confirm against an official page before recording.
- **Facebook pages/groups:** "日本音樂會香港情報站 / Japanese Music Live in Hong Kong"
  (facebook.com/JPmusicliveHK), animeeventshk group, "HK JPOP/JROCK FANS".
- **Promoter Facebook pages:** ED Production (EDProductionHK), Nobody Production,
  UnUsUaL Entertainment (UnusualHK), Sunny Side Up (光尚娛樂).
- **Instagram:** promoter/venue accounts and hashtags (#jpoplivehongkong,
  #香港演唱會, #animeeventhk); follow artists' official accounts.
- **X/Twitter:** search "香港 ライブ", "来港", "Asia Tour", "香港站", "Jpop live Hong Kong";
  follow artists' + promoters' official accounts.
- **Discord:** HK/Asia JP-live servers (invites usually shared via the FB/IG pages above).
- **TikTok:** HK/Taiwan/SEA fan communities repost date announcements & calendars.

> **Lesson:** big English/Chinese aggregators skew toward arena-scale pop/rock and
> miss smaller **anisong / seiyuu livehouse** shows. For full coverage, also poll
> artist official sites, eventernote, and niche promoters (ED Production, etc.)
> directly.

> Aggregators, fan databases, and social posts are **leads**, not facts. Every lead
> must be confirmed against an A- or B-tier (official / ticketing) source — with a
> live official announcement or on-sale ticket page — before it becomes a `confirmed`
> entry. Handles/URLs above are starting points; verify each is genuine and current.

## 2. Collection workflow

```
Discover (search sources)
   → Capture raw lead (inbox)
      → Verify against official / ticketing source
         → Normalize into event schema (see AGENTS.md)
            → Deduplicate
               → Record + set status
                  → Schedule re-check
```

### Step 1 — Discover
Run periodic searches across the sources in Section 1. Useful query patterns:
- `"<artist>" 香港 2026`, `"<artist>" Hong Kong live 2026`
- `<artist> 海外公演 / アジアツアー / Asia tour 2026`
- `香港 <artist> concert / live / ファンミ`
- **Search by anime IP, not just artist** — for currently-airing/hot works, search the
  franchise + `香港`/`Hong Kong` (e.g. `ガールズバンドクライ 香港 2026`,
  `Girls Band Cry Togenashi Togeari Hong Kong`). Announcements are often framed around
  the anime, and the band/seiyuu name alone can miss them.
- Venue calendars filtered to the target month.
- Ticketing platform searches for the target month's date range.

> **Do not rely on ticketing platforms alone.** Shows in the `announced` state (date +
> venue set, tickets not yet on sale) are under-indexed on art-mate/timable/UUTIX and in
> general web search. Always also poll **ACG-news feeds (ACGEvent/shadowzo), eventernote,
> and 巴哈姆特 per-anime boards** for these, and record them as `announced` with ticket
> info TBA rather than skipping them. Re-check later for the on-sale page.

### Step 2 — Capture raw lead
Log every candidate immediately (even unverified) with: artist, claimed date,
claimed venue, the source URL, and where you found it. Use the `inbox_entries`
table (Section 4) as a staging area.

### Step 3 — Verify
Promote a lead only when confirmed by an **official** or **ticketing** source.
Check: correct artist, correct city (Hong Kong), date within Aug 2026, real venue,
and that it isn't an old/cancelled announcement. Keep the confirming URL.

### Step 4 — Normalize
Map into the event schema defined in `AGENTS.md` (artist, artist_type, event_title,
date, time, venue, city, ticket_info, source, status, notes). Dates/times in **HKT**.

### Step 5 — Deduplicate
Match on **artist + date + venue**. Merge duplicates; keep the most authoritative
source and the most complete details.

### Step 6 — Record & status
Write the entry to the dataset and set `status`
(`announced` → `confirmed` → `on_sale` → `sold_out` / `postponed` / `cancelled`).

### Step 7 — Re-check
Announcements change. Re-poll sources on a schedule (Section 3) and update `status`,
times, and ticket info instead of deleting entries.

---

## 3. Cadence

- **Weekly sweep** of official sites, promoter/venue calendars, and ticketing
  platforms for new announcements.
- **Daily light check** as August 2026 approaches (T-8 weeks onward), since on-sale
  dates and sell-outs move fast.
- **Event-triggered**: whenever a lead appears in community channels, verify within
  a day or two.

## 4. Staging table (leads inbox)

Use the `inbox_entries` table to stage unverified leads before they become dataset
entries. Suggested columns:

| column        | meaning                                             |
|---------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| id            | unique id                                            |
| artist        | claimed artist/band name                             |
| claimed_date  | claimed date (YYYY-MM-DD or range)                   |
| claimed_venue | claimed venue                                        |
| source_url    | where the lead was found                             |
| source_tier   | `official` / `ticketing` / `promoter` / `aggregator` / `community` |
| status        | `new` / `verifying` / `confirmed` / `rejected` / `duplicate` |
| notes         | anything relevant                                    |
| found_at      | timestamp                                            |

Verified `confirmed` leads graduate into the main dataset per `AGENTS.md`.

## 5. Verification & quality rules

- **Two-signal rule for high confidence:** an event is `confirmed` when an official
  announcement **and** a ticketing/venue listing agree (or one strong official
  source with explicit HK date + venue).
- **Never invent** dates, prices, or venues. If unknown, leave blank and note it.
- **Prefer primary sources**; downgrade `status` to `announced` when only community
  sources exist and flag the uncertainty in `notes`.
- **Scope filter:** discard anything not in Hong Kong or outside Aug-Nov 2026 (unless
  scope is explicitly expanded).
- **Timezone:** all dates/times in **HKT (UTC+8)**.
- **Keep provenance:** every dataset entry retains at least one source URL.

## 6. Legal / ethical

- Only collect **publicly available** information.
- Respect site **robots.txt** and terms of service; avoid aggressive scraping —
  prefer official feeds/pages and reasonable request rates.
- Link back to original sources; do not republish paywalled or private content.
- Record facts (who/what/when/where/tickets), not copyrighted media.
